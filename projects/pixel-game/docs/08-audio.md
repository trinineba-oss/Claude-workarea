# 8. Audio

## Decided

There is **no sound or music in the game yet**. The direction from the design:

- **Music:** chiptune-ish with **steelpan** timbres. Soca and calypso rhythms for lively areas,
  a dreamy, slightly detuned pan lullaby for the overworld, parang flavour for a festive area.
