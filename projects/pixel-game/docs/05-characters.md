# 5. Characters

Data lives in `data/characters.json` (people), `data/dogs.json` (top dogs) and
`data/dialogue.json` (what they say). Which picture draws whom: `data/looks.json`.

## Main cast

| Character | Role | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Chad** | The hero | Washes up at the wharf; cutlass; speaks or silent (undecided) |
| **Evey** | Chad's beagle, companion | Based on the owner's dog who passed away. Wakes Chad on the wharf, follows him, bites enemies, chases thieves, takes orders (Sic 'em, Stay/Come, Fetch, Dig), fights turn-based duels and levels up. Stays in the dream at the end |

| **The Gyro God** | Final "boss" | Sleeps inside a giant roti on San Fernando Hill; you wake him, not fight him |

## Enemies and bosses

Night bandits (snatch and run), corbeaux (swoop), soucouyant fireballs (night, beaches),
crabs, callaloo blobs. Bosses: **Big Blue Crab** (mini-boss, Temple 1), **Callaloo Cauldron**
(Temple 1 boss). Ideas: mosquitoes, jumbies, steups teens, the maco.

## Character card template

Copy this for each character you want to flesh out:

```md
### Name
- Role / where they are:
- Look (clothes, colours, one memorable detail):
- Personality in three words:
- How they talk (sample line):
- What they want:
- Day / night routine:
- Likes / dislikes (for gifts):
- Secret or quest they give:
```
