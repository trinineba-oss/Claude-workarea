# 4. Assets: art still needed

Everything except Chad and Evey's example sheets is placeholder art generated by the tools in
`tools/`. Tick items off as real art arrives.

## Sheet format for characters

For Chad and Evey's full set of animations, use the prompts in
[Character art prompts](15-character-art-prompts.md) (one row of frames per sheet). The 4×2
pose layout below still works for quick single poses.

The format that worked for Chad and Evey: **one image, 4 poses across, 2 rows, on a flat grey
background**, figures with a dark outline and no overlapping.

| Row | Pose 1 | Pose 2 | Pose 3 | Pose 4 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Facing camera | 3/4 view | Back | Back 3/4 |
| 2 | Walk step 1 | Walk step 2 | Action 1 | Action 2 |

Action poses: attack / hurt for people and enemies, sit / bark for dogs. More walk steps per
direction (4–8) look smoother. Same light direction, outline and size for the whole cast.
Put the sheet in `art_src/painted/<name>_poses.jpg` and add the character's layout to
`tools/painted_sheets.py`; the tool cuts it into a game sheet (transparent frames of 128 px).

Small icons: **square, transparent PNG, 128×128**, one object, no background.
