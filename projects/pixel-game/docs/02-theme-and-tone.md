# 2. Theme and tone

## Decided

- **Parody with a straight face.** Everyone treats the sandwich quest as sacred. The joke is epic
  seriousness applied to everyday life, not constant winking.
- **Link's Awakening whimsy.** Quirky townsfolk, talking animals, a dreamlike world, short punchy
  dialogue, small sad-sweet moments between the jokes.
- **Affectionate and local.** It celebrates Trinidad; it doesn't caricature it. Jokes punch at
  situations (traffic, gossip, queues, pepper, everybody's mother saying come home before dark),
  never at groups of people.
- **Respect the player's time.** Short rooms, generous saves, no grinding.
- **Original.** Borrow structure, never Nintendo's names, characters, art or music, and never
  real brands or real people. Shops and characters get invented names.
- **Night is comic-dangerous**, not grim: a gloating bandit with a loot bag, no weapons or real
  violence. Stick to the lights.
- **Evey** carries the heart of the story: a real dog who passed away, remembered warmly. Her
  scenes stay gentle; she is never hurt badly or played for cheap sadness.

Themes underneath the jokes: hunger and home; the small rituals of a place (liming, the food
trucks after dark); a dream where the people you miss are still with you.
