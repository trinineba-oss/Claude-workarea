# 1. Storyline

## Decided

**Premise.** Chad and his beagle Evey wash up at **San Fernando Wharf** in a strange, dreamlike
San Fernando, Trinidad. The **Legendary Lamb Gyro** waits at the top of **San Fernando Hill**.
Only the **Gyro God**, asleep inside a giant roti, can release it, and it takes **four sacred
seasonings** to wake him.

**The twist (the ending).** It was all a dream. Chad dozed off by the gyro truck on **Lady
Hailes Avenue, by Cross Crossing**, waiting for his order. The vendor shakes him awake ("Chad!
Yuh gyro ready!"). He gets an ordinary, delicious gyro. **Evey stays in the dream**: she isn't
there when he wakes. Bittersweet, then funny; credits roll over the busy food stretch.

The four seasonings can come from temples *or* from the life sim (foraging, fishing, cooking,
friendships). Seasoning 1 is the Sacred Chadon Beni ("for Chad").

**Doner Dread**, a smug fast-food baron, chases the same prize and turns up at each temple one
step ahead, gloating.
