# 11. Roadmap

Each milestone ends in a playable build with passing tests.

## Done

1. **Foundation:** rooms, camera, collisions, touch controls, saving.
2. **Combat:** cutlass, doubles for health, knockback, pothounds and corbeaux, pickups.
3. **World and dialogue (first pass):** the wharf, the road up, Cross Crossing, Lady Hailes
   Avenue food trucks, people and dialogue from data files.
4. **Temple 1, Callaloo Cave:** keys, doors, push blocks, Big Blue Crab, coconut boomerang.
5. **Boss and slice ending:** Callaloo Cauldron, the Sacred Chadon Beni, Doner Dread's entrance.

Also done: day and night, night dangers, foraging, fishing, Evey and her orders, dog duels, the
2.5D look (3D lighting, props, pixel characters).

## Next (suggested order)

1. **Art direction**: choose one style for the cast ([Art style](03-art-style.md)) and replace
   the cast art ([Assets](04-assets.md)).
2. **Polish**: sound and music, title screen, settings, credits, phone testing.
3. **Story**: seasonings 2–4 and temples 2–4 outlined ([Storyline](01-storyline.md)).
4. **World**: Harris Promenade hub, High Street and market.
5. **Life sim**: cooking, friendships, vendor schedules, selling.
6. **Temples 2–4.**
7. **Finale**: San Fernando Hill, the Gyro God, the wake-up ending, credits.
