[AAA](https://www.instagram.com/challenger.aaa/), https://pixel-boy.itch.io/ninja-adventure-asset-pack

Used here, renamed but unchanged:

- `sfx/`: sound effects (swings, hits, impacts, breaking, fire, water, coins, power-ups, a dog,
  menu moves) from `Audio/Sounds/`.
- `music/`: `village.ogg` (36 - Village), `wharf.ogg` (39 - Boat), `fight.ogg` (17 - Fight).
- `fx/`: slash arc and spin sheets (`FX/Slash`), smoke puff (`FX/Smoke/Smoke`), leaf, wood,
  grass and flame particles (`FX/Particle`).
- `font/pixel.ttf`: the pack's NormalFont.
